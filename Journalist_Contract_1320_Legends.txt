JOURNALIST CONTRACT — 1320 LEGENDS
==================================================

THE POSITION
------------
The Community Journalist position is a voluntary content role within the 1320
Legends game. Journalists are responsible for covering in-game events, community
news, player spotlights, and game updates for the 1320 Legends community board
and any official social channels designated by the 1320 Legends team.

Journalists do not receive complimentary memberships or premium cars as part of
this role. Journalists will be held to the same behavioral standards as all
other players and are expected to maintain accuracy, fairness, and
professionalism in all published content.

Abuse of your position as a Community Journalist will not be tolerated. Abuse
includes, but is not limited to:

  - Publishing false, misleading, or defamatory information about players,
    staff, or the game.
  - Using your access to early information to gain an unfair in-game advantage.
  - Sharing confidential information provided by the 1320 Legends team before
    it is cleared for public release.
  - Harassing players or staff members under the guise of reporting.
  - Plagiarizing content from other community members or outside sources.

As a Community Journalist, you serve as the voice of the 1320 Legends
community. Your content shapes how players understand and experience the game.
It is essential that you always act with integrity and represent the community
in a positive and constructive manner.


DUTIES
------
  - Write and publish community news articles, patch note summaries, and event
    recaps to the official 1320 Legends community board.
  - Conduct player and staff spotlights/interviews (with the subject's consent).
  - Cover in-game tournaments, events, and competitions in a timely manner.
  - Fact-check all content before publishing to ensure accuracy.
  - Correct or retract any published content found to contain errors.
  - Maintain a consistent publishing schedule as agreed upon with the 1320
    Legends team (minimum one article per week to remain active status).
  - Communicate story ideas and questions to the 1320 Legends team using the
    contact information provided in the journalist tools portal.


CONTENT STANDARDS
-----------------
All published content must adhere to the following standards:

  - Accuracy: All facts, figures, and quotes must be verified before publishing.
  - Fairness: Coverage must be balanced and not targeted at harming any
    individual player or group.
  - Originality: All written content must be the journalist's own original work.
    Quoting or referencing outside sources requires proper attribution.
  - Language: Content must be free of profanity, hate speech, sexual content,
    or any language that violates the 1320 Legends Terms of Service.
  - Approval: Any article that covers unannounced game features, staff matters,
    or sensitive community issues must be reviewed and approved by the 1320
    Legends team before publishing.

The 1320 Legends team reserves the right to edit, remove, or retract any
published content that does not meet these standards. Repeated violations may
result in removal from the Journalist program.


TOOLS
-----
  1. Community Board access — Journalists are granted elevated posting
     permissions on the official 1320 Legends community board, allowing them
     to publish featured articles and pinned announcements.

  2. Early patch note access — Journalists may receive early access to patch
     notes and update information under embargo, strictly for the purpose of
     preparing timely community coverage. Embargo terms must be respected.

  3. Journalist portal — A web-based portal for submitting articles for review,
     communicating with the 1320 Legends team, and accessing press resources
     such as official screenshots, logos, and game media.

  4. Direct communication with the 1320 Legends team — use ATTN: Press in your
     subject line when emailing the team.


IN-GAME WEEKLY COMPENSATION & POINTS
--------------------------------------
In recognition of the time and effort Community Journalists contribute to
keeping the 1320 Legends community informed and engaged, active journalists will
receive the following weekly in-game compensation:

  Weekly Cash Reward:
    - Staff Writer:          $30,000 in-game cash per week
    - Featured Journalist:   $60,000 in-game cash per week

  Weekly Points Reward:
    - Staff Writer:          300 Press Points per week
    - Featured Journalist:   600 Press Points per week

  Bonus Points:
    - Each published article (approved and posted):  +100 Press Points
    - Player/Staff spotlight published:              +150 Press Points
    - Event recap published within 24 hrs of event:  +200 Press Points

  Point Milestones & Perks:
    Points accumulate over time and unlock the following rewards:

    ┌──────────────────┬─────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
    │  Points Earned   │  Reward                                         │
    ├──────────────────┼─────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
    │  1,500 pts       │  Exclusive Press decal / livery                 │
    │  3,500 pts       │  Rare community car unlock                      │
    │  7,500 pts       │  Premium garage slot + bonus cash ($150,000)    │
    │  15,000 pts      │  "Press Pass" badge + journalist title in-game  │
    └──────────────────┴─────────────────────────────────────────────────┘

  Conditions for Receiving Weekly Compensation:
    - Journalist must have published at least one approved article during the
      week to qualify for compensation.
    - Compensation is forfeited for any week in which content violations are
      confirmed.
    - Compensation is distributed every Monday for the prior week's activity.
    - Journalists under review or suspended from the program are not eligible
      to receive compensation during that period.

  Note: In-game compensation and points have no real-world monetary value and
  are provided solely as recognition of journalist service within the game.


GUIDELINES FOR JOURNALIST ACTIVITY
------------------------------------
We understand that producing quality content takes time and effort. We
appreciate every article, recap, and spotlight you create for the 1320 Legends
community. We ask that active journalists publish a minimum of one article per
week to maintain active status. If you anticipate a period of inactivity, please
notify the team in advance. If at any time you no longer wish to serve as a
Community Journalist, please notify us and we will return your account to normal
player status.


LEGAL / OTHER ISSUES
--------------------
All content published under the 1320 Legends Journalist program becomes the
property of BricKHouse Software and 1320 Legends upon publication. Journalists retain
credit for their work but grant BricKHouse Software a non-exclusive, royalty-free license
to use, reproduce, and distribute the content across any 1320 Legends platform.

Journalists must not publish content that could expose BricKHouse Software or 1320
Legends to legal liability, including but not limited to defamation, copyright
infringement, or disclosure of confidential information.

All journalists are required to provide a street mailing address so that a
printed copy of this contract may be sent for signature and return. Journalists
who are under 18 must have their parent or legal guardian sign the form as well.
