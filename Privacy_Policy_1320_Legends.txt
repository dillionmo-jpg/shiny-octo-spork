PRIVACY POLICY — 1320 LEGENDS
==================================================
Effective Date: June 1, 2026
Last Updated:   June 1, 2026

This Privacy Policy ("Policy") explains how BricKHouse Software ("we," "us," or "our")
collects, uses, stores, and shares information when you access or play 1320
Legends (the "Game") or visit any associated websites or platforms. By using
the Game, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with
this Policy.

If you do not agree with this Policy, please do not access or use the Game.


1. INFORMATION WE COLLECT
--------------------------
We may collect the following types of information:

  1.1  Information You Provide Directly
       - Account registration information (username, email address, password).
       - Mailing address (required for community role holders — Moderators,
         Guides, Journalists — for contract delivery purposes only).
       - Communications you send to us, including support requests, feedback,
         and email correspondence.
       - Content you submit to the Game, including chat messages, forum posts,
         guide content, and articles.

  1.2  Information Collected Automatically
       - Device information (device type, operating system, browser type).
       - IP address and approximate geographic location (country/region level).
       - Game activity data (cars owned, race results, in-game purchases,
         session length, chat activity).
       - Log data (access times, pages viewed, errors encountered).
       - Cookies and similar tracking technologies (see Section 6).

  1.3  Information from Third Parties
       - If you access the Game through a third-party platform (e.g., a social
         network or app store), we may receive certain profile information from
         that platform, consistent with your privacy settings on that service.


2. HOW WE USE YOUR INFORMATION
-------------------------------
We use the information we collect for the following purposes:

  2.1  To provide, operate, and maintain the Game and its features.
  2.2  To create and manage your account.
  2.3  To process in-game transactions and deliver Virtual Items.
  2.4  To distribute in-game weekly compensation and points to community role
       holders (Moderators, Guides, Journalists).
  2.5  To communicate with you, including responding to support requests,
       sending game updates, and delivering policy notices.
  2.6  To send a printed copy of community role contracts to Moderators,
       Guides, and Journalists via postal mail (mailing address is used for
       this purpose only and is not shared with third parties for marketing).
  2.7  To enforce our Terms of Service and maintain community safety, including
       investigating reports of misconduct, scamming, or policy violations.
  2.8  To analyze usage patterns and improve the Game's features and
       performance.
  2.9  To detect, prevent, and respond to fraud, security incidents, and
       technical issues.
  2.10 To comply with applicable legal obligations.


3. HOW WE SHARE YOUR INFORMATION
----------------------------------
We do not sell your personal information. We may share your information in the
following limited circumstances:

  3.1  Service Providers
       We may share information with trusted third-party service providers who
       assist us in operating the Game (e.g., hosting providers, analytics
       services, email delivery services). These providers are contractually
       required to keep your information confidential and use it only for the
       purposes for which it was shared.

  3.2  Community Role Administration
       Usernames and role-related activity data for Moderators, Guides, and
       Journalists may be visible to other community role holders and
       administrators for operational purposes (e.g., moderator notes,
       activity logs). Mailing addresses of role holders are accessible only
       to authorized BricKHouse Software staff for contract delivery purposes.

  3.3  Legal Requirements
       We may disclose your information if required to do so by law, or if we
       believe in good faith that such disclosure is necessary to: (a) comply
       with a legal obligation; (b) protect and defend the rights or property
       of BricKHouse Software; (c) prevent or investigate possible wrongdoing in
       connection with the Game; or (d) protect the safety of players or the
       public.

  3.4  Business Transfers
       In the event of a merger, acquisition, or sale of all or a portion of
       our assets, your information may be transferred as part of that
       transaction. We will notify you via a prominent notice on the Game or
       by email before your information becomes subject to a different privacy
       policy.

  3.5  With Your Consent
       We may share your information with third parties when you explicitly
       consent to such sharing.


4. DATA RETENTION
-----------------
We retain your personal information for as long as your account is active, or
as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes described in this Policy. When you
delete your account, we will delete or anonymize your personal information
within a reasonable timeframe, except where we are required to retain it for
legal or compliance purposes (e.g., records of moderation actions, transaction
history).

Chat messages and community posts may be retained for moderation and safety
purposes even after account deletion.

Mailing addresses provided by community role holders will be retained only for
the duration of their role and will be securely deleted within 90 days of role
termination.


5. DATA SECURITY
----------------
We implement reasonable technical and organizational security measures to
protect your personal information from unauthorized access, disclosure,
alteration, or destruction. These measures include:

  - Encrypted storage of passwords (hashed and salted).
  - Secure HTTPS connections for all web-based interfaces.
  - Access controls limiting who within BricKHouse Software can access personal data.
  - Regular security reviews of our systems and practices.

However, no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is
100% secure. We cannot guarantee the absolute security of your information.
If you suspect your account has been compromised, contact us immediately.


6. COOKIES & TRACKING TECHNOLOGIES
------------------------------------
We use cookies and similar technologies (such as local storage and pixel tags)
to:

  - Keep you logged in to your account.
  - Remember your preferences and game settings.
  - Analyze Game traffic and usage patterns.
  - Detect and prevent fraudulent or unauthorized activity.

You may configure your browser to refuse cookies; however, doing so may affect
certain features of the Game or associated web platforms. We do not currently
respond to "Do Not Track" signals from browsers.


7. CHILDREN'S PRIVACY
----------------------
The Game is intended for users aged 13 and older. We do not knowingly collect
personal information from children under the age of 13. If we become aware that
we have collected personal information from a child under 13 without verifiable
parental consent, we will take immediate steps to delete that information.

If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child under 13 has provided us
with personal information, please contact us immediately at the email address
listed in Section 11.

For users aged 13–17, we encourage parents and guardians to review this Policy
and to be involved in their child's use of the Game. Community role holders
(Moderators, Guides, Journalists) who are under 18 must have their contracts
co-signed by a parent or legal guardian.
