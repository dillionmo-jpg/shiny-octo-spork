GUIDE CONTRACT — 1320 LEGENDS
==================================================

THE POSITION
------------
The Community Guide position is a voluntary educational role within the 1320
Legends game. Guides are experienced players who create, maintain, and publish
tutorials, how-to guides, FAQs, and strategy content to help new and existing
players get the most out of their 1320 Legends experience.

Guides do not receive complimentary memberships or premium cars as part of this
role. Guides will be held to the same behavioral standards as all other players
and are expected to be patient, helpful, knowledgeable, and respectful in all
interactions.

Abuse of your position as a Community Guide will not be tolerated. Abuse
includes, but is not limited to:

  - Publishing intentionally incorrect or misleading game information that
    causes other players harm or disadvantage.
  - Using your Guide status to promote personal gain, solicit in-game items,
    or pressure other players.
  - Disparaging other guides, moderators, or staff members in your published
    content or public communications.
  - Sharing unverified tips or strategies as though they are confirmed facts
    without clearly labeling them as opinion or community theory.

As a Community Guide, you serve as a trusted resource for 1320 Legends players
of all skill levels. Your content directly shapes how new players learn the game
and how experienced players refine their strategies. Act with patience,
thoroughness, and a genuine desire to help.


DUTIES
------
  - Write, publish, and maintain beginner and advanced player guides on the
    official 1320 Legends community board and/or guide portal.
  - Create FAQ content addressing common questions asked in the community.
  - Update existing guides promptly following game updates, patches, or balance
    changes that affect guide accuracy.
  - Answer player questions in designated help channels and guide-related
    threads in a courteous and thorough manner.
  - Flag outdated or incorrect community guides to the 1320 Legends team for
    review or removal.
  - Communicate guide topic requests and community feedback to the 1320 Legends
    team using the contact information provided in the guide tools portal.


CONTENT STANDARDS
-----------------
All guide content must adhere to the following standards:

  - Accuracy: All game mechanics, values, and strategies described in guides
    must be tested and verified before publishing.
  - Clarity: Guides should be written in clear, easy-to-understand language
    accessible to players of all experience levels.
  - Currency: Guides must be updated within a reasonable timeframe following
    any game update that affects their accuracy. Outdated guides must be
    clearly labeled as such pending revision.
  - Originality: All guide content must be the guide author's own original
    work. Content sourced from other guides or outside sources requires proper
    attribution.
  - Language: Content must be free of profanity, hate speech, or any language
    that violates the 1320 Legends Terms of Service.

The 1320 Legends team reserves the right to edit, remove, or archive any guide
content that does not meet these standards. Repeated violations may result in
removal from the Guide program.


TOOLS
-----
  1. Guide portal — A web-based portal for submitting, editing, and managing
     published guides. Guides are granted elevated community board permissions
     to publish pinned tutorials and help articles.

  2. Test environment access — Where available, Guides may be granted limited
     access to test environments to verify game mechanics before publishing
     guide updates following major patches.

  3. Community help channels — Guides are given a designated Help channel
     presence and may be listed in the in-game help directory as a trusted
     resource for player questions.

  4. Direct communication with the 1320 Legends team — use ATTN: Guide Team
     in your subject line when emailing the team regarding guide matters.


IN-GAME WEEKLY COMPENSATION & POINTS
--------------------------------------
In recognition of the time and effort Community Guides contribute to educating
and supporting the 1320 Legends player community, active guides will receive
the following weekly in-game compensation:

  Weekly Cash Reward:
    - Community Guide:       $35,000 in-game cash per week
    - Senior Guide:          $70,000 in-game cash per week

  Weekly Points Reward:
    - Community Guide:       350 Guide Points per week
    - Senior Guide:          700 Guide Points per week

  Bonus Points:
    - New guide published (approved):                +125 Guide Points
    - Existing guide updated after a patch:          +75 Guide Points
    - FAQ article published:                         +100 Guide Points
    - Guide rated "Helpful" by 10+ players:          +50 Guide Points

  Point Milestones & Perks:
    Points accumulate over time and unlock the following rewards:

    ┌──────────────────┬─────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
    │  Points Earned   │  Reward                                         │
    ├──────────────────┼─────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
    │  1,750 pts       │  Exclusive Guide decal / livery                 │
    │  4,000 pts       │  Rare community car unlock                      │
    │  8,500 pts       │  Premium garage slot + bonus cash ($175,000)    │
    │  20,000 pts      │  "Mentor" badge + Guide title in-game           │
    └──────────────────┴─────────────────────────────────────────────────┘

  Conditions for Receiving Weekly Compensation:
    - Guide must have published or meaningfully updated at least one guide, or
      actively participated in designated help channels during the week.
    - Compensation is forfeited for any week in which content standards
      violations are confirmed.
    - Compensation is distributed every Monday for the prior week's activity.
    - Guides under review or suspended from the program are not eligible to
      receive compensation during that period.

  Note: In-game compensation and points have no real-world monetary value and
  are provided solely as recognition of guide service within the game.


GUIDELINES FOR GUIDE ACTIVITY
------------------------------
We understand that creating thorough, accurate guides takes significant time and
research. We appreciate the effort you put into making 1320 Legends more
accessible and enjoyable for everyone. We ask that active guides contribute at
least one new or updated guide per month, or maintain regular activity in
community help channels to retain active status.

If you anticipate a period of inactivity, please notify the team in advance. If
at any time you no longer wish to serve as a Community Guide, please notify us
and we will return your account to normal player status. Your published guide
content will remain on the platform unless you specifically request its removal.

--------------------
All guide content published under the 1320 Legends Guide program becomes the
property of BricKHouse Software and 1320 Legends upon publication. Guides retain credit
for their work but grant BricKHouse Software a non-exclusive, royalty-free license to use,
