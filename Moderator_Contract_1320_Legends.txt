MODERATOR CONTRACT — 1320 LEGENDS
==================================================

THE POSITION
------------
The moderator position is a voluntary position within the 1320 Legends game.
Moderators do not receive complimentary memberships or premium cars.
Moderators will be held to the same behavioral standards as all other users.
Abuse of your power as moderator will not be tolerated. Abuse includes, but is
not limited to:

  - Banning people for no reason.
  - Disrespecting fellow players, administrators, or moderators.
  - Un-banning friends who were banned by other moderators, administrators, or
    the system.
  - Using your position as moderator to intimidate, harm, or otherwise cause
    unwarranted disruption to other players.

As a moderator, you will serve as a link between 1320 Legends game players and
the BricKHouse Software team. It is important that you always act in a courteous and
professional manner.


DUTIES
------
  - Answer basic questions in the chat rooms on how to play the game.
  - Help resolve disputes between players, including allegations of scamming.
  - Warn players who violate the terms of service.
  - Communicate ongoing problems to the 1320 Legends game staff using the email
    address provided within the mod tools interface.

To help you perform the above duties, we have provided you with a set of tools.
You should use these tools to help you perform the duties listed above.


TOOLS
-----
  1. Web-based mod tools website — found at: http://nitto1320legends.com/admin
     Use this site to ban/un-ban players, remove yourself from other players'
     block lists, send notes to other moderators, and delete chat rooms.

  2. In-game moderator tools — automatically appear in the chat room, allowing
     you to quickly and efficiently ban players for given periods of time.

  3. Direct communication with the 1320 Legends team — use ATTN: Admin in your
     subject line.


REASONS FOR BANNING
--------------------
You may ban players for any of the following reasons. However, before doing so
you MUST first warn the player:

  - Excessive abusive language (profanity, cybersex, racial slurs, etc.).
  - Attempting to scam or cheat other players.
  - Attempting to impersonate a mod or admin.
  - Flooding of the chat rooms or message boards.
  - Posting cheat and/or scam sites.


GUIDELINES FOR BANNING
-----------------------
You MUST follow these guidelines when moderating the chat rooms to ensure that
each player is treated fairly.

  First Offense:      Warn the player publicly.
  Subsequent Offenses: Ban the player from the chat and the game for a period
                       of time, depending on the offense.

Length of Ban:
  - Abusive language, flooding:                    Chat Ban — up to 48 hours
  - Attempting to scam or cheat other users:       Maximum Chat / Game Ban
  - Impersonation of an Admin or Moderator:        Maximum Chat / Game Ban
  - Cybersex:                                      Maximum Chat / Game Ban

Repeat offenders are given less leeway; however, make sure that you tell the
offender why they are being banned and for the specific length of time.


IN-GAME WEEKLY COMPENSATION & POINTS
--------------------------------------
In recognition of the time and effort moderators contribute to maintaining a
safe and enjoyable community, active moderators will receive the following
weekly in-game compensation:

  Weekly Cash Reward:
    - Standard Moderator:    $50,000 in-game cash per week
    - Senior Moderator:      $100,000 in-game cash per week

  Weekly Points Reward:
    - Standard Moderator:    500 Mod Points per week
    - Senior Moderator:      1,000 Mod Points per week

  Point Milestones & Perks:
    Points accumulate over time and unlock the following rewards:

    ┌──────────────────┬─────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
    │  Points Earned   │  Reward                                         │
    ├──────────────────┼─────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
    │  2,500 pts       │  Exclusive Moderator decal / livery             │
    │  5,000 pts       │  Rare community car unlock                      │
    │  10,000 pts      │  Premium garage slot + bonus cash ($250,000)    │
    │  25,000 pts      │  Legendary status badge + title in-game         │
    └──────────────────┴─────────────────────────────────────────────────┘

  Conditions for Receiving Weekly Compensation:
    - Moderator must have been active (logged in and performed at least one
      moderation action) during the week.
    - Compensation is forfeited for any week in which the moderator is found
      to have abused their position.
    - Compensation is distributed every Monday for the prior week's activity.
    - Moderators who are suspended or under review are not eligible to receive
      weekly compensation during that period.

  Note: In-game compensation and points have no real-world monetary value and
  are provided solely as recognition of moderator service within the game.


GUIDELINES FOR MODERATOR ACTIVITY
-----------------------------------
We realize that you lead a busy life, and would like to let you know that we
appreciate you taking the time out of your schedule to help keep the 1320
Legends community safe and fun. While we specifically look for active players,
we have no "minimum time required" or specific schedule that you need to be on
for. Our goal is that at any given time there will be at least one moderator
logged into the system, and that during peak periods this will increase to at
least 3 moderators. To that end, we simply ask that you try to be consistent
in the amount of time you choose to spend on moderating, and let us know if you
plan to make a dramatic change. If at any time you no longer wish to be a
moderator, please let us know and we will set your account back to normal status.


LEGAL / OTHER ISSUES
--------------------
As some of you may be aware, we had some issues with moderators in the past who
did not perform their duties as expected and had to be removed. Some of those
former moderators then made it their mission to try to disrupt the game, often
times by illegal means. Therefore, we will require all moderators to provide a
street mailing address where we can send a printed copy of these moderator
terms, to be signed and returned to us. Moderators who are under 18 must have
their parent or legal guardian sign the form as well.
